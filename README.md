Two lines to my past self: the first warns against saying yes to the night shift on March 3rd, when my mother calls at 2 a.m. from the hospital hallway; the second says why that moment mattered — it was the last time I heard her voice, and I spent the whole night stocking shelves while she said goodbye to a nurse instead.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the two lines to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) as UTF-8 with no trailing newline.
